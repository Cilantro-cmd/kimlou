About Me

Kim Lou

I'm a UI designer with a background in law. After training as a candidate attorney at a global firm, I moved into design — bringing the same habits of structured thinking, careful reading, and attention to edge cases to a different kind of problem.

My work focuses on turning complex flows into interfaces that feel simple and consistent. I build with design systems and reusable components, and I think about how every screen connects to the next.

I'm looking for a team where clarity matters and where I can do work that holds up to scrutiny.
